## Small categories

On the test set, one ticket is worth 100 ÷ n points of a category's accuracy. The brief asks the report to say when a category is too small for the per-category limit (top model − 15 points) to be a fair test. Our working cut-off (a team choice, not from the brief): a category is too small when a single ticket is worth more than 10 points.
