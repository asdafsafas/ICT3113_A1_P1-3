## Derived observations (analysis, not in the source)
> [!key-insight] Small models collapse onto one label
> From recall × n ÷ precision:
> - **0.5B answered "Credit card" for ~155 of 195 tickets (~79%)**, and Credit reporting for ~39.
> - **1B answered "Credit reporting" for ~121 of 195 (~62%).**
>
> So the prediction that the 0.5B would over-use Credit reporting was wrong about *which* label it defaults to. See [[Predictions vs Outcomes]].

- The 7B's Money transfer errors are mostly misses (precision 95%, recall 67%). Those tickets go elsewhere, consistent with the predicted Bank account ↔ Money transfer confusion (Bank account precision is 76%).
- For [[Cascade Classification]] in A2, the 1B has **high precision but low recall** on Mortgage, Credit card, Bank account and Money transfer. A category-based acceptance rule would accept only a small share of tickets. See [[Assignment 2 Planning]].
