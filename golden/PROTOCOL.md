## Purpose

This protocol defines how the team labels complaint tickets for the golden test set, which is used to measure each candidate model's classification accuracy. The dataset's own labels were chosen by consumers and are unreliable, so every golden-set label comes from this protocol instead.

## Procedure

1. Every ticket is labelled by **at least two team members independently**, without conferring, using the labelling app (`labeler/`). Each person's labels are saved to their own sheet.
2. The app shows only the ticket text and row number. The dataset's original labels are hidden so they cannot bias the labeller.
3. Labellers do not ask any AI model which category a ticket belongs to. Looking up unfamiliar terms or company names is allowed.
4. For each ticket the labeller records:
   - **Label:** one of the seven categories below, or *None / unclear*
   - **Also fits** (optional): a second category the ticket could reasonably belong to
   - **Reasoning:** a short explanation, especially for doubtful cases
5. The protocol is not changed while a labelling round is in progress. Gaps are noted in the reasoning and resolved afterwards.
6. After labelling, agreement is measured (Cohen's kappa per pair of labellers; Fleiss' kappa for groups of three or more). Every disagreement is discussed and resolved, with the reason recorded. If a group cannot agree, a team member who did not label those rows acts as tie-breaker.
7. Where a disagreement reveals a gap in this protocol, a rule is added and the version is bumped (see the revision log).
8. A ticket may be **excluded** from the golden set if it cannot be assigned to one of the seven categories; each exclusion and its reason is recorded.

## Categories

| # | Category | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Credit reporting** | Problems with a credit report or the credit bureaus (Equifax, Experian, TransUnion): wrong or outdated entries, disputes the bureau didn't handle, accounts from identity theft appearing on the report, improper use of a report, credit repair or monitoring services. |
| 2 | **Debt collection** | How someone is trying to collect a debt: collector calls, threats, harassment, being chased for a debt the person doesn't owe or has already paid, requests to verify the debt, lawsuits. Applies whatever the debt was for (medical, credit card, etc.). |
| 3 | **Mortgage** | Home loans: applying, closing, making payments, escrow, loan changes, foreclosure, home equity loans and home equity lines of credit (HELOCs). |
| 4 | **Credit card** | A credit card account: billing disputes, unauthorised charges, fees and interest, credit limits, rewards, applications, the account being closed. |
| 5 | **Bank account or service** | Checking and savings accounts: opening or closing them, deposits and withdrawals, overdraft fees, frozen accounts or held funds, debit and ATM card problems. |
| 6 | **Consumer loan** | Instalment loans that aren't mortgages: car loans and leases, personal loans, instalment loans, payday loans and student loans (including student loan servicers such as AES, Navient, Nelnet). |
| 7 | **Money transfer or service** | Sending or receiving money: wire transfers, payment apps (PayPal, Cash App, Zelle, Venmo), international remittances, money orders, and scams carried out through these services. |

**None / unclear** (labelling only): The ticket fits none of the 7 categories, or is too vague to decide. Explain why in the reasoning. This is not a final golden-set label: the team resolves these later.

## Deciding between categories

**Tie-break rule:** Label the product or company the complaint is mainly about, not everything it mentions.

**Edge-case rules:**

- Credit reporting vs Debt collection: decide by who the complaint is against. A collector's conduct (contact, cease-and-desist, failing to validate or give notice of a debt) is Debt collection, even if the fix asked for is removal from the credit report. A demand to the bureaus about their report or dispute handling is Credit reporting. (e.g. rows 3025, 3039, 3055, 3071, 3072)
- Credit reporting vs the underlying product: a dispute with a credit bureau (or a specialty agency such as SageStream) is Credit reporting whatever account is on the report. A dispute with the lender or servicer about what it reports or how it applied payments is that lender's product. (e.g. rows 3011, 3023, 3034, 3050, 3080, 3081)
- Debt collection needs collection activity by a collector or about a defaulted debt. Fees or interest on a person's own credit card are Credit card. (e.g. rows 3001)
- Unauthorised debit-card transactions or wire transfers out of a bank account (fraud, account takeover) are Bank account or service. Use Money transfer or service when the complaint is about a transfer service or app the person used. (e.g. rows 3009, 3049)
- Label the product, not the type of company: a bank can issue a mortgage or a store credit card (e.g. Wells Fargo loan modification -> Mortgage; Comenity Capital Bank -> Credit card). (e.g. rows 3017, 3030)
